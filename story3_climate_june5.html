WMO: 2026 Temperatures on Track but Extreme Weather Surging

The World Meteorological Organization confirmed global temperatures are tracking within Paris Agreement's 1.5°C target for the first five months of 2026.

However, the report warns of a sharp increase in heat waves, floods and storms across the Asia-Pacific region — a sign that extreme weather risks continue to grow.
