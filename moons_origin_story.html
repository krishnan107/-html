🌕 The Moon's Origin Story

Did you know the Moon may have been born from a cosmic sacrifice?

Around 4.5 billion years ago, a Mars-sized planetary body called Theia collided with the early Earth. This dramatic event, known as the Giant Impact Hypothesis, reshaped our planet and led to the formation of the Moon.

Theia was completely destroyed in the collision, but its material became part of both Earth and the Moon. Scientists believe traces of this ancient world still exist within them today.

To uncover Theia's origins, researchers analyzed isotopic fingerprints from Earth rocks, Apollo Moon samples, and meteorites.

Their findings suggest that Theia formed in the inner Solar System and likely originated closer to the Sun than Earth.

Key Takeaway:
The Moon may be the lasting legacy of an ancient planet that gave up its existence in a collision that helped shape Earth itself.
