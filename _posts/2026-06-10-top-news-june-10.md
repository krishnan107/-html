The world has plenty to talk about today — from Lionel Messi's magical return on the pitch to a major immigration bill clearing the U.S. House.

## ⚽ Messi Scores as Argentina Preps for World Cup

In what may be his final World Cup warm-up ever, Lionel Messi came off the bench in the 70th minute against Iceland and immediately made his mark. His first touch drew a foul, and he stepped up to bury the penalty — his **117th international goal** — as Argentina cruised to a 3-0 win in Auburn, Alabama.

Valentin Barco and Thiago Almada also got on the scoresheet. Argentina head into the 2026 FIFA World Cup, which officially kicks off June 11 on home soil across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, in commanding form. Uzbekistan, Jordan, Cape Verde, and Curaçao will all make their World Cup debuts this tournament.

## 🇺🇸 House Passes $70 Billion Immigration Package

The U.S. House passed a sweeping immigration enforcement bill Tuesday by a razor-thin 214–212 margin. The $70 billion package locks in ICE funding through the end of the Trump presidency and includes major border security provisions. Democrats strongly opposed the bill, calling it excessive — but it now heads to the Senate.

## 🗳️ California Governor's Race Set

California's jungle primary is done. Democrat **Javier Becerra** and Republican **Steve Hilton** (backed by President Trump) will face off in November for the state's governorship. Hilton's advance is seen as a strong test of Trump's endorsement power in the nation's most populous state.

## 🏀 Trump at the NBA Finals

President Trump attended Game 3 of the NBA Finals at Madison Square Garden — Knicks vs. Spurs — and received a notably mixed reaction from the sold-out crowd, a stark contrast to his reception at the College Football Playoff national title game earlier this year.

## 🇬🇧 Belfast Protests After Stabbing

UK leaders called for calm after anti-immigration protests erupted in Belfast following the arrest of a Sudanese man accused of a violent stabbing. Officials urged restraint as tensions ran high across the city.
